Main redacts secrets and copies every required fix into the assigned owner's changed Brief as structured data. After Main parent-verifies a fix, it creates fresh attempt/round targets and captures a new complete bounded snapshot immediately before dispatching each required fresh Round 2 reviewer. Do not dispatch Round 3 unless Round 2 identifies a genuine Blocker.

Recompute and dispatch the next complete ready set even while an unrelated unit remains failed. A failed implementation rejoins dispatch only through `RETRY_READY`, using the same requested selector and a fresh attempt-specific ledger; a stale or findings review rejoins only as a fresh worker session with a new gates ledger, new round report, and current anchored manifest plus complete bounded immutable review snapshot. Main never fixes implementation, review report, or gate files and never supplies old artifacts to workers. This preserves barriered waves without global failed-wave blockage, eager replenishment, or eager verification.
